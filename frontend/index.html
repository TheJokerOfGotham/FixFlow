How escrow works

  1. Fund - you deposit exactly the agreed amount.
  2. Accept - the assigned technician accepts before the deadline (or declines → full refund).
  3. Complete - the technician marks the job done before the deadline.
  4. Review - you approve (payment released) or dispute within the review period.
  5. Arbitrate - an arbitrator chooses: pay technician, refund tenant, or 50/50 split.

Safety nets

  • Cancel any time before acceptance → full refund.
  • Deadline missed → you can reclaim the full deposit.
  • You stay silent after completion → technician can claim after the review period.
  • No arbitrator acts in time → either party can apply the 50/50 default split.

Role management

Only the contract administrator can change roles. The contract enforces this independently of the UI.
